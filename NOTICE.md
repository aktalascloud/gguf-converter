# Sources and third-party notices

The converter source is distributed under AGPL-3.0. Its Artius decoder adapts `MossConvert.py` from the KoboldCpp Media source. The `vendor/hf` directory contains the pinned KoboldCpp Hugging Face converter and GGUF Python modules. The converter copy disables tokenizer remote code. Its upstream license and GGUF-Python license are in that directory. [KoboldCpp Media source](https://github.com/aktalascloud/koboldcpp-media-pipeline/tree/f387ee7b08317340ab2890c31383d0d6dcc12539) · [KoboldCpp source](https://github.com/LostRuins/koboldcpp/tree/2d469743d30d09737633607071fe83dc2283bfec).

The executable embeds the exact CPU tools from llama.cpp and stable-diffusion.cpp, including their GGML code and image/video libraries. MIT and BSD notices and the WebM patent grants are in `licenses/`. The llama.cpp archive also carries its LLVM OpenMP notice. [llama.cpp b12211](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/tree/b12211) · [stable-diffusion.cpp fc350be](https://github.com/leejet/stable-diffusion.cpp/tree/fc350be) · [sd.cpp GGML source](https://github.com/leejet/ggml/tree/d803c238f67915fb825a9aa4df27b99356d51597) · [libwebp source](https://github.com/webmproject/libwebp/tree/0c9546f7efc61eac7f79ae115c3f99c91c21c443) · [libwebm source](https://github.com/webmproject/libwebm/tree/5bf12267eea773a32fcf4949de52b0add158a8d5).
